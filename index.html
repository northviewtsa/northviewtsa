Engineering the Future at Northview High School

The Technology Student Association (TSA) is a national organization of students engaged in STEM. At Northview, our chapter competes in dozens of events from robotics and coding to engineering design and leadership, preparing members for careers in technology and beyond.

What Is TSA?

A chapter of 60+ members competing, building, and leading.

The Technology Student Association is the only national student organization devoted exclusively to the needs of students interested in science, technology, engineering, and mathematics (STEM). TSA has over 250,000 members in middle and high schools across the country

We also run VEX Robotics and Electric Car year-round, hovercraft challenge, a summer STEM Academy for middle schoolers, and community service opportunities.
