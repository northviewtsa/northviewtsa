Competitions

From regional Tech Day to the National Conference — Northview TSA competes at every level.

TSA competition events span a broad range of STEM disciplines including software development, CAD and structural design, robotics, bioengineering, video production, and more. Members may compete in up to 2 events (ECAR members: ECAR + 2).

Regional · Fall

Chapter Officer Retreat for Excellence (CORE)

Core, located at The Kaplan Mitchell Retreat and Conference Center, is designed to provide chapter officers with an opportunity to develop interpersonal and leadership skills, gain necessary knowledge to foster a successful year for the chapter.
