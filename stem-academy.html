STEM Academy

A summer enrichment program bringing STEM education to middle schoolers in our community.

About the Program

Northview TSA's STEM Academy is a week-long summer program designed to introduce middle school students to engineering, coding, and robotics through hands-on activities led by TSA members.

Part of the proceeds are donated to a nonprofit focused on STEM accessibility and educational equity. The program is run entirely by student volunteers from Northview TSA and VEX Robotics.

The goal isn't just revenue — it's getting TSA and VEX known to middle schoolers and parents, building community connections, and earning chapter recognition awards.

Program Overview

Duration5 days
Grade Levels6th – 8th grade
Cost$5 per day per student
Hours7 hours per day
FormatMorning: lessons · Afternoon: hands-on activity
Sign-UpStudents can register for individual days

Sample Daily Schedule

Day 1

Structural Design

Lesson: structural design principles. Activity: spaghetti towers and catapult challenge.

Day 2

Aerodynamics & Automotive

Lesson: intro to aerodynamics and basic CAD concepts. Activity: drag racing and vehicle design.

Day 3

Coding Lab

Lesson: coding fundamentals and web design basics. Activity: hands-on coding challenge.

Day 4

Robotics Workshop

Lesson: intro to robotics and VEX systems. Activity: VEX-based challenge.

Day 5

Showcase Day

Students present their projects from the week. Parents are welcome. TSA and VEX club introduction.
