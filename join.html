Frequently Asked Questions

What is TSA?

The Technology Student Association is a national career and technical student organization for students in STEM programs. We compete in 50+ events at the state and national levels.

Do I need prior experience?

No! TSA welcomes students at all experience levels. Many of our most accomplished members started with zero background in engineering or coding. There's lots of opportunities to learn from more experienced members throughout the year.

What's the time commitment?

General meetings are every 2 weeks. STEM Academy and community service are optional but earn you points toward honor cords. Most of the time will be spent by yourself or with your team in your own time preparing for competitions.

What are VEX and ECAR?

VEX Robotics and ECAR (Electric Car) are subclubs within Northview TSA. When you fill out the membership form, you can indicate interest in one or both, and both will have their own interest meetings soon after TSA's.

How do I earn honor cords?

Honor cords are awarded to seniors who accumulate enough points through meetings, competitions, and service. Thresholds: Bronze = 100 pts, Silver = 175 pts, Gold = 250 pts.
